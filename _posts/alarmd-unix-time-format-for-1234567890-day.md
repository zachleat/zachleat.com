In honor of [1234567890 day][1], I’ve added the Unix time format to [ALARMd][2].

 [1]: http://www.1234567890day.com/
 [2]: http://www.alarmd.com/

Hit **Options**, then the **Clock Format tab** to change to Unix Time. Enjoy!
